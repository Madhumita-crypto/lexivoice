Product notes

Learning that speaks your language.

LexiVoice is a lightweight voice-first vocabulary learning prototype. It is designed to make practising a new word feel approachable, quick, and useful in everyday life.

How it works

  • Discover: Learn a daily word with its meaning, pronunciation, and a contextual example.
  • Practise: Speak or type your own sentence and receive immediate, rule-based guidance.
  • Reinforce: Test recall with short quizzes and save words for revision.
  • Track: See saved words and quiz performance on your device.

Prototype transparency

  • Speech recognition uses your browser's built-in Web Speech API when supported. Microphone access requires permission and a secure hosted connection (HTTPS).
  • Spoken responses use browser text-to-speech. Voice availability depends on your device and browser.
  • Vocabulary coaching and sentence feedback are powered by a small local demo dataset and simple checks, not a live generative AI model.
  • Your saved words and quiz stats are stored locally in this browser. No account or backend is used.

This prototype demonstrates the core learning loop and interaction design. A production version could add adaptive spaced repetition, richer pronunciation scoring, learner profiles, and an AI conversation engine.
